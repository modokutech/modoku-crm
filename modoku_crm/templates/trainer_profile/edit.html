Roughly how much to write

Guidance only, not enforced — for a profile that fits the layout cleanly without looking empty or overflowing:

Background60–90 words (one solid paragraph)
Experience90–150 words (one or two short paragraphs)
Academic Qualifications3–6 entries — degree, institution, year, location; no prose
Professional Certifications5–20 short one-line entries — long lists are fine, they're compact
Professional Experience3–6 roles, each a one-line title plus one short bullet
Areas of Expertise (sidebar)6–16 short phrases (2–4 words each)
Companies Trained (sidebar)practically unlimited — splits across pages on its own if long
Custom sections4–10 bullets each, kept under ~8 words per bullet
